Entry for make.fun's **Monday Morning Punchlines**: one joke about Monday
mornings, at most 200 characters. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Why am I so energetic on Monday mornings? I take the 401(k) to work — it drives me straight to retirement.

The joke runs on two layers: the 401(k) as the ride that gets you to work,
and as the thing that drives you straight to retirement.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
